#My favorite series is an anime series entitled **That time i got reincarnated as a slime**

The main plot is about a guy named *Mikami Satoru* who got stabbed and reincarnated as a slime in another world. He was then named by the storm dragon *Veldora* as *Rimuru Tempest*. From then, he started to build a nation where he can live happily with his friends.

My top 5 favorite characters in order are:
1. Diablo
2. Rimuru
3. Zegion
4. Carrera
5. Ultima
